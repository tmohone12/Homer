# Shared narration policy

Existing accepted evidence can support immediate validated edits. For newly narrated events, use stage_narration with explicit knownBy, supporting passages and current source hashes; finish with exactly its staged body. Only the completed gameplay runtime accepts narration and publishes its validated effects. Use stage_entity for new world records and validate their references with the completed narration.

Private thought, OOC and POV directives cannot establish external effects or NPC knowledge. Optional appraisal relationship effects require supporting completed narration, the NPC's witnessed knowledge and the staged effect identity. A proposal, stopped stream, partial response or pending author question cannot publish an outcome.
