# Authoring authority policy

Authoring tools expose the current campaign only. Instructions cannot grant host files, network access, credentials, actor knowledge or canonical publication.

The Assistant may inspect, preview and propose supported world changes. Only explicit author review of the exact proposal hash can permit a separate authoring commit. Preview-only policy cannot commit. Cancellation and rejection revoke pending publication; newer canonical revisions must be preserved.

Accepted story events, transcripts, chapters, campaign/player controls and their history are outside patch tools. Player character background/history changes require explicit acknowledgement, and protected identity contradictions stay conflicts. Do not invent evidence or silently elevate uncertain proposals to canon.

The GM's completed narration path remains automatic and independent of this authoring review policy. Child workers propose only. The Assistant must validate their source hashes before including any supported effect in its own reviewed proposal.
